(04) Process, in pieces

Every visual is a stack of decisions.

Pull a collage apart and you find the thinking: a field of colour, a found texture, a portrait, a living detail. I design websites the same way — in layers that each earn their place.

My way of getting things done

Fast and transparent: the path to a website that represents your brand in the best of light is about four weeks. One clear step at a time, so you always know where your project stands.

  1. Content, structure and user journeys mapped into wireframes we can test before a single pixel is polished.

    Week 01

  2. Art direction, typography, colour and motion — the visual layer that gives your brand its voice.

    Week 02

  3. Semantic, accessible and fast builds in Webflow or hand-written code, tuned for every screen size.

    Week 03

  4. Analytics, SEO foundations and a hand-over session — plus ongoing care once you’re live.

    Week 04
